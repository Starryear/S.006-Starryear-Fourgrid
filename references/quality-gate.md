# Quality gate

Inspect the finished PNG once as a combined final review. In that single pass, confirm the overall quartet at fit-to-screen and zoom only the central junction when a visible edge or alpha concern exists. Do not repeat the review when no concrete defect is present.

## 1. Geometry and source lock

- Master is exactly 2:3 and reads instantly as a 2×2 quartet.
- Four cells are equal vertical 2:3 quadrants with a thin, quiet, source-derived seam.
- Panel 1 uses actual source pixels after deterministic crop, scale, or padding.
- Primary subject, viewpoint, light, count, geometry, and important photographic accidents remain intact.
- The central motif does not cover the protected evidence.

## 2. Four-state progression

- Panel 1: literal evidence.
- Panel 2: recognizable poetic memory with the surreal event beginning.
- Panel 3: structural rhythm without literal redraw.
- Panel 4: bold nonliteral soul with the surreal event released.
- Literal detail decreases while field scale, direction, rhythm, and tension increase.
- Panels share source DNA but are not filtered copies or unrelated designs.

## 3. Central junction collage

- One subject-specific motif links the central cross and normally occupies only 3–8% of the master.
- A real subject fragment or recognizable silhouette transitions into its principal line, then into rhythm.
- The line visibly agrees with the subject's actual gesture, architecture, growth, shoreline, reflection, or movement.
- It touches or visually addresses at least two generated quadrants; three or four is acceptable when still restrained.
- It reads as a hinge between visual states, not a sticker, badge, flower ornament, tape scrap, fifth panel, or random scribble.
- Alpha edges are clean: no rectangular matte, white/black halo, jagged pixels, blur fringe, or drop shadow.

## 4. Surrealism and source logic

- There is one controlled impossible behavior, not a pile of effects.
- The event transforms evidence that already exists in the photograph.
- It remains readable and poetic without introducing unrelated fantasy content.
- The result is slightly uncanny, never theatrical, kitsch, mystical, or cinematic.

## 5. Clean surface and color

- Palette comes entirely from the source and keeps its temperature and luminance hierarchy.
- Accent remains scarce; no invented fashionable accent spreads through the sheet.
- Negative space is clean and actively shaped.
- Paper or ink texture is low-amplitude and local, not a full-sheet dirt overlay.
- There are no stains, dirty antique paper, heavy specks, muddy color mixing, overprinted blacks, uncontrolled splatter, dense fog, glow, vignette, or grunge.
- Unless the source is intrinsically very dark, near-black occupies less than roughly 15% of Panels 2–4.

## 6. Resolution and edge quality

- Final delivery is PNG in RGB or sRGB, preferably 3072×4608; minimum 2048×3072.
- No visible JPEG ringing, pixelation, blocky masks, posterization, banding, or low-resolution upscaling artifacts.
- At least one focal contour per generated panel is crisp and readable.
- Soft ink diffusion retains controlled tide lines or feathering rather than uniform blur.
- Fine lines survive thumbnail view and do not break into noise at full size.
- Any generated anatomy, architecture, text, or repeated subjects remain structurally credible.

## 7. Hard rejection list

Reject before delivery if any appears:

- regenerated or visibly stylized Panel 1;
- four filters, four copies, or four unrelated artworks;
- central collage missing, arbitrary, oversized, or covering the anchor;
- thick black grid, framed cards, rounded panels, or scrapbook treatment;
- generic bamboo, crane, mountain, moon, temple, portal, calligraphy, seal, or fantasy symbol;
- default beige/ivory/gold/gray-green palette unrelated to the source;
- dirty parchment, heavy grain, fake aging, messy splatter, muddy wash, or excessive halftone;
- illegible typography, fake metadata, logo, signature, or watermark;
- tiny centered motifs and accidentally empty corners;
- broad blur used to hide weak structure;
- output below minimum resolution or visibly compressed.

## Stopping rule

If Panel 1 fails, fix deterministic assembly; never regenerate it. If only the junction fails, replace or resize the junction asset and reassemble. If one generated panel fails source logic, cleanliness, or progression, regenerate only that panel once with the failed invariant stated explicitly. Do not restart the complete sequence, and do not perform another review cycle unless a targeted repair was actually made. After one targeted regeneration still fails, keep the strongest valid panel set and report the remaining limitation instead of accumulating more decorative effects.
