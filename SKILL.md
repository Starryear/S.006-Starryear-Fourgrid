Do not use additional captions, place descriptions, camera information, quotations, legends, color swatches, logos, signatures, watermarks, or promotional text.

PROGRESSION TEST
The panels should progress as follows:

TOP LEFT:
The literal world.

TOP RIGHT:
The recognizable poetic memory of the world.

BOTTOM LEFT:
The rhythm and structure beneath the world.

BOTTOM RIGHT:
The emotional and spatial soul released from the world.

The sequence must become more abstract, but not emptier.

The amount of literal description decreases.

The strength of rhythm, movement, field, scale, and visual tension increases.

QUALITY CONTROL
Before finalizing, verify:

1. Is Panel 1 the faithful original photograph?
2. Does every generated panel use only Image 1 as its content source?
3. Has the visual soul of Image 1 been identified and carried through all panels?
4. Does each quadrant contain a complete composition?
5. Are the generated panels active across most of their width and height?
6. Do elements enter, cross, or relate to panel edges?
7. Is Panel 2 recognizable without becoming a miniature redraw?
8. Does Panel 3 behave like a full abstract rhythm or visual score?
9. Does Panel 4 express the deepest force of the image through large fields and strong directional elements?
10. Does abstraction increase without causing the compositions to become empty?
11. Can every important mark be traced to a source fact, relationship, rhythm, or color role?
12. Are the dominant axis, movement, contrast, intervals, depth, overlap, and asymmetry preserved?
13. Do the four panels share one color system and one visual language?
14. Is the single title grounded in the source image?
15. Does the entire 2 × 2 composition feel like one finished artwork?

STRICTLY AVOID
A tiny centered motif surrounded by excessive blank space; empty or unfinished corners; four unrelated abstract designs; four photographic filters; repeated copies of the source scene; miniature scene reconstruction; literal vector tracing; generic modernist decoration; arbitrary circles and lines; infographic layout; perfectly equal spacing; invented symmetry; unrelated symbols; invented subject matter; unrelated colors; decorative filler; weak full-panel composition; excessive text; duplicated titles; logos; signatures; watermarks; frames; cards; mockups; drop shadows; tape; torn-paper collage; strong gradients; glow; haze; vignette; artificial aging; or heavy background texture.

FINAL OUTPUT
Return one polished vertical 2:3 four-panel composition only.

The finished work must feel like:

ONE PHOTOGRAPH  
DECONSTRUCTED INTO FOUR STATES  
WITH THE SOUL OF THE ORIGINAL IMAGE  
FLOWING THROUGH EVERY PANEL.
