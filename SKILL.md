# 【S-006】Starryear-2x2

Create one finished vertical 2:3 artwork from exactly one authorized source photograph. Keep the actual photograph unchanged in the top-left cell; derive the other three cells exclusively from its colors and observable relationships. Preserve four fixed transformation roles rather than any tested subject or palette: `Photographic Evidence → Dissolving Memory → Point-Line Score → Surreal Relational Afterworld`. The final cell must keep the source's relationship map while replacing literal objects with a new source-derived spatial vocabulary.

## Workflow

1. Require one user-supplied photograph. Inspect subject, dominant force, fixed and temporary elements, movement, enclosure, contrast, anchor, repetitions, density, open space, and warm/cool balance; reduce them internally to one short `SOUL STATEMENT`.
2. Lock a source palette: dominant atmospheric color, dark structural color, light source color, secondary color, and smallest important accent. Every visible color in Panels 2–4, including their background, must derive from this palette.
3. Build a precise vertical 2:3 master as four equal vertical 2:3 cells. Rebuild Panel 1 from the current upload every run: use that photograph's actual pixels, never a remembered example or generated stand-in, and permit only proportional crop or source-derived padding needed to fit. When the generator cannot guarantee pixel fidelity, composite the current source into Panel 1 after generation.
4. Make Panel 2 a 70–80% abstract poetic memory using organic, wet, broken, and dissolving fields: one large atmospheric field, one cropped edge mass, one long axis, distributed rhythm, and no more than two incomplete identity fragments. The fragments anchor memory but may not recombine into a simplified scene.
5. Make Panel 3 a point-line-dominant score in a deliberately different formal language. Preserve the photograph's principal trajectories, decisive intersections, meaningful counts, pauses, and density changes; map repeated units into unequal solid nodes, hairlines, short segments, and grouped intervals. The original subject silhouette must disappear. Permit only restrained translucent source-color wash bands and broken residues behind the notation so it belongs to the same material family as Panels 2 and 4; points and lines must still carry most of the information. Prohibit flower-like radial marks, splatter, broad wet brushwork, and dissolved object silhouettes.
6. Make Panel 4 a source-traceable surreal afterworld. Preserve the source map—relative positions, scale hierarchy, overlap, depth, count rhythm, dominant direction, enclosure, and light-dark pressure—but replace literal objects with nonliteral source-derived volumes, chambers, folds, portals, seams, terrains, or other adaptive spatial equivalents. It must feel recognizably born from the photograph without reading as a stylized repaint, and it must not collapse into arbitrary hard geometry.
7. Return exactly one polished final artwork with no visible typography anywhere.

## Guardrails

- Panel 1 must not be redrawn, regenerated, recolored, graded, stylized, extended, retouched, simplified, or filtered.
- Never hard-code a tested photograph's subject, palette, crop, count, direction, landmark, or `SOUL STATEMENT`. Re-run source analysis from zero for every new upload, including Panel 1 placement.
- Image 1 is the sole source of content and color. A method reference, if supplied, may teach only abstraction logic—never palette, tonality, texture, subject, exact shapes, layout, title, or mark placement.
- Do not default to ivory, cream, beige, gold, gray-green, warm paper, or global desaturation. Such hues are allowed only when visibly derived from the source.
- Preserve the source's dominant-color area ratio and luminance hierarchy. Do not dilute a dominant blue, dark, warm, or saturated field into generic pale paper; generated panels must remain immediately chromatically related to Panel 1.
- Activate upper, middle, lower, left, center, right, edges, and corners in every generated cell; breathing room must be shaped by nearby forces, never feel abandoned.
- Use one primary mark family and no more than two supporting families. Preserve irregular, observed rhythm; avoid infographic neatness and decorative filler.
- Panel 3 must not collapse into an arbitrary Cartesian grid. Its line directions, intersections, node counts, pauses, and landmark cue must be mapped from observable source geometry.
- Panel 3 fails if it still reads first as a tree, flower, person, building, animal, vehicle, or other source-object silhouette; recognition must come from mapped trajectory, count, interval, and density instead.
- Panels 2 and 3 must pass a silhouette-and-mark test: even in grayscale and without labels, Panel 2 reads as organic atmospheric memory while Panel 3 reads as restrained structural notation. They may share a faint source-derived wash DNA, but not the same dominant mark family, edge behavior, or compositional logic.
- Panels 2 and 4 must not collapse into the same wet-brush solution. Panel 2 is delicate, translucent, fragmentary, layered, and partially erased. Panel 4 is more opaque, spatial, dimensional, silhouette-led, and organized as an inhabitable impossible field. At thumbnail size and in grayscale, their edge behavior and depth logic must remain unmistakably different.
- Panel 3 must visibly prioritize point-line rhythm: points and lines carry most of its information and spatial movement, with at least three source-derived trajectories, multiple unequal node groups, and deliberate gaps. Do not let pale bands, washes, or empty background become the main event.
- Do not invent unrelated subjects, symbols, narratives, palettes, symmetry, or generic ink-landscape clichés.
- Panel 4 must perform `relationship preservation + object replacement`: retain the source's spatial grammar while changing the identity of its objects. A stylized repaint, enlarged object, generic brush field, or unrelated geometric design fails.
- Never hard-code tulips, flowers, plants, white-and-green palettes, hollow shells, glowing chambers, or any tested composition. Terms such as chamber, fold, portal, seam, and terrain describe transformation functions only; choose equivalents from the current source.
- Exclude all letters, words, numbers, dates, captions, titles, archive notation, logos, signatures, watermarks, mockups, cards, frames, shadows, tape, torn-paper collage, glow, vignette, heavy aging, pseudo-text, and extra panels.
