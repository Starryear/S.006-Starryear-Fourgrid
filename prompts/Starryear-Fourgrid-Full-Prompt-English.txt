# Starryear-Fourgrid Full Prompt

Treat exactly one photograph uploaded or authorized by the user as the sole source of content, structure, color, light, and emotional tension. Create one clean, restrained, subtly surreal vertical 2:3 photographic-art quartet: Photographic Evidence at top left, Poetic Distillation at top right, Rhythmic Structure at bottom left, and Abstract Soul at bottom right. Connect the central cross with only one motif derived from a subject fragment and its characteristic line. Do not apply four filters to the photograph, and never redraw the top-left source image.

## 1. Role of the input photograph

The input must be exactly one photograph supplied or authorized by the user.

It serves two roles:

1. The original photograph in the top-left cell. Use actual source pixels. Permit only deterministic proportional scaling, the smallest necessary crop, or padding with a low-information light color sampled from the source to fit a vertical 2:3 cell. Do not redraw, outpaint, replace, retouch, recolor, filter, or reconstruct it.
2. The sole evidence for the other three cells and the central motif. Every shape, axis, rhythm, color, material change, and subtle surreal behavior must trace to an observable fact in the photograph.

Do not introduce a second image, unsupported object, symbol, scene, color, or narrative.

## 2. One-pass internal observation

Inspect and judge the photograph once. Do not output the analysis. Lock the decisions and reuse them in every generation step rather than analyzing the source repeatedly.

Determine internally:

- the primary subject and the face, gesture, animal identity, architectural anchor, reflection, overlap, or count that must remain protected;
- the minimum identity contour and the most characteristic subject line;
- the main horizontal, vertical, diagonal, arcing, radial, or enclosing axis;
- movement direction, real repeated count, interval changes, dense zone, pause, and largest negative-space shape;
- camera viewpoint, near/middle/far hierarchy, perspective convergence, and light direction;
- dominant color, dark structural color, light color, secondary color, and one scarce accent;
- warm/cool balance, brightest area, darkest area, and contrast hierarchy;
- one SOUL STATEMENT formed by two observed forces;
- one controlled surreal event;
- one central junction motif.

The controlled surreal event may only change the behavior of existing evidence: a subject line crossing materials, a reflection detaching, perspective folding once, a shadow separating slightly, repeated subjects entering a measured path, or photographic matter migrating into print and chromatic ink. Use exactly one event. Do not add portals, planets, mystical symbols, decorative animals, or cinematic spectacle unless they are present in the source.

## 3. Four-state progression

All four cells must share the same subject line, direction, rhythm, color roles, and emotional tension. Literal detail decreases from cell to cell while structural and nonliteral force increases.

### Panel 1: Photographic Evidence, top left

Place the actual photograph. Preserve subject identity, true count, viewpoint, light, shadow, reflection, spatial depth, occlusion, color balance, and meaningful photographic accidents. Protect decisive evidence from the central motif. Use no text.

### Panel 2: Poetic Distillation, top right

Keep the scene recognizable through relationships rather than full depiction. Use one broad atmospheric field from the source light or dominant color, one characteristic subject line, one observed rhythm, one or two minimum identity cues, and one scarce accent event. Begin the controlled surreal event quietly. Route one contour toward the lower-left central junction. Activate upper, middle, lower, left, center, and right zones while remaining clean. Do not create a tiny icon, thumbnail illustration, or watercolor copy.

### Panel 3: Rhythmic Structure, bottom left

Stop depicting literal objects. Turn axes, intervals, actual count groups, depth, reflection, pauses, and density changes into a complete visual score. Select one primary mark family from long lines, irregular bars, broken bands, partial arcs, or repeated modules, with no more than two supporting families. Receive the subject line from the upper-right central junction and let it cross upper, middle, and lower zones before splitting into measured rhythm. Preserve observed irregularity. Avoid infographics, perfect grids, equal spacing, decorative patterns, and generic geometry.

### Panel 4: Abstract Soul, bottom right

Release the SOUL STATEMENT in the strongest and least literal state. Use one source-colored field occupying about 40%–65% of the cell, one main line or arc crossing most of its width or height, one large mass entering from an edge, two to five medium structures, a few rhythmic traces, and one concentrated source accent. Let the controlled surreal event reach its clearest form. Receive the same subject line near the upper-left central junction, then let it expand, fold, cross, separate, or dissolve. Retain one crisp identity contour so the result remains specific to this photograph.

## 4. Central junction motif

Place one transparent-background subject-line collage over the exact four-cell crossing. It should behave like a material hinge:

photographic or printed fragment → precise subject contour → two or three sparse rhythmic traces → abstract release.

Use only one small crop, silhouette, void, or material fragment from the source plus its most identifiable line. Limit color to the dark structural source color and at most one scarce source accent. Cover about 3%–8% of the master, retain wide transparent gaps, and visually address at least two generated cells.

The motif may touch a nonessential edge of Panel 1 but must not cover a face, decisive gesture, animal identity, architectural anchor, or other protected evidence. It must not resemble a sticker, badge, floral ornament, seal, circular logo, taped paper, portal, target, or fifth cell. Reject rectangular mattes, white or black halos, jagged alpha, glow, and drop shadows.

## 5. Composition and canvas

Return one vertical 2:3 RGB or sRGB PNG, preferably 3072×4608 and never below 2048×3072. Use four equal cells, each also vertical 2:3, in the fixed order.

Use a 2–6 px divider sampled from a light or low-information source color. Do not add an outer frame, rounded cards, thick border, picture frame, tape, mockup, or dimensional shadow.

Panels 2–4 must each be complete compositions. Let at least one form relate to an upper edge, one to a side edge, one enter the lower zone, and one meaningful event occupy the center. Activate roughly 55%–75% of the composition through fewer, larger, better-related forms. Negative space must be shaped by adjacent direction and pressure rather than becoming an abandoned corner.

## 6. Adaptive subject handling

- People and crowds: preserve gesture, interval, direction, occlusion, and count. Avoid malformed faces, separate circular heads, cartoon limbs, and regular capsule figures.
- Architecture: preserve one to three identity cues, perspective pressure, representative negative space, and contour. Omit windows, masonry, carving, and other surface detail.
- Plants, clouds, water, snow, and organic groups: organize large masses through observed growth, gathering, dispersal, flow, reflection, and density. Do not add generic ink-landscape symbols.
- Animals: preserve anatomical direction, pose, count, and minimum identity contour. Do not invent animals or change species.
- Small objects: retain minimum recognizability through two or three positionally and proportionally accurate planar marks, without realistic highlights or internal construction.

## 7. Color and mark system

Every visible color in Panels 2–4 and the central motif must be sampled or perceptually derived from the source. Preserve the original roles: dark colors organize structure, light colors create breath and depth, secondary colors separate layers, and the accent remains small. Do not default to beige, ivory, gold, gray-green, warm paper, or a Japanese-neutral palette. Keep cool photographs cool, nearly monochrome photographs nearly monochrome, and tiny saturated colors tiny.

Choose one primary mark family and no more than two supporting families across the work. Allowed materials include precise ink-like contour, restrained dry print, transparent chromatic wash, controlled tide edges, sparse halftone, and flat source-colored fields. Keep at least one crisp focal edge in each generated cell. Softness must describe distance, reflection, or material migration, never hide weak structure with uniform blur.

## 8. Material and cleanliness

Use texture only locally and at low amplitude. Keep large areas clean. Unless the source is intrinsically very dark, keep near-black below about 15% of Panels 2–4.

Prohibit full-sheet grain, dirty parchment, stains, fake aging, muddy color mixing, uncontrolled splatter, heavy black pooling, charcoal fog, global vignette, glow, scan marks, compression artifacts, pixelation, banding, and blocky transparency.

## 9. Typography

Use no text by default. Prohibit numbers, dates, serials, place labels, color swatches, legends, signatures, logos, watermarks, and pseudo-text. Only when the user explicitly requests typography and it materially improves the editorial composition may one exact two-to-five-word English title and at most one tiny archive number be added. Add reliable text deterministically after generation and never place it over Panel 1.

## 10. Fast production and single review

1. Inspect the source once and create one locked evidence record.
2. Generate Panels 2–4 and the transparent junction as separate assets, but schedule independent generations in parallel when the tool supports it. Reuse the same locked source facts for every request.
3. Assemble deterministically. Never ask the generator to redraw the finished quartet in one pass.
4. After assembly, perform one combined review of ratio, order, source pixels, four-state progression, central alpha edges, palette, cleanliness, and minimum resolution.
5. When no concrete defect is visible, stop immediately. Do not repeat zoom checks, repeat source analysis, or regenerate without a reason.
6. If a defect exists, fix assembly or regenerate only the failed cell or junction once. Never restart the full set, and never regenerate Panel 1.

## 11. Output limits

Return only one completed PNG. Do not output analysis, alternatives, prompt explanations, separate panels, or extra copy unless the user explicitly asks.

Strictly exclude four filters, four redundant images, four unrelated artworks, a redrawn or recolored Panel 1, a missing or oversized junction, generic bamboo, cranes, mountains, moons, temples, seals, or calligraphy, unsupported fantasy content, thick black grids, card layouts, dirty paper, heavy grain, muddy watercolor, dense splatter, blur used to hide errors, dead corners, low-resolution edges, generated pseudo-text, logos, signatures, and watermarks.
